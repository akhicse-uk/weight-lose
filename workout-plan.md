# Workout Plan

An easy, beginner-friendly routine. Needs no gym or equipment, about 30–40 minutes a day.

## Every day

- **Walk 7,000–10,000 steps.** Start with what you can manage and add 1,000 steps a week.
- **5 minutes of stretching** before bed.
- **A 10–15 minute walk after lunch and dinner.** It's one of the best ways to lower blood
  sugar after a meal, which matters for South Asians, who have a higher risk of type 2
  diabetes. An easy time to fit it in is after Maghrib or Isha.

## Weekly schedule

| Day | Workout | Time |
|-----|---------|------|
| Mon | Brisk walk + **Strength A** | 30 + 15 min |
| Tue | Brisk walk | 40 min |
| Wed | Brisk walk + **Strength B** | 30 + 15 min |
| Thu | Brisk walk or cycling | 40 min |
| Fri | Brisk walk + **Strength A** | 30 + 15 min |
| Sat | Long walk, swim or sport you enjoy | 45–60 min |
| Sun | Rest: light stroll and stretching only | 20 min |

**Brisk** means you can talk but couldn't sing.

## Strength A (full body)

Do 3 rounds. Rest 60 seconds between rounds.

| Exercise | Reps |
|----------|------|
| Squats (or sit-to-stand from a chair) | 12 |
| Wall or knee push-ups | 10 |
| Glute bridges | 15 |
| Plank | 20–30 sec |

## Strength B (full body)

Do 3 rounds. Rest 60 seconds between rounds.

| Exercise | Reps |
|----------|------|
| Reverse lunges (each leg) | 8 |
| Bent-over row with water bottles or a backpack | 12 |
| Bird-dog (each side) | 8 |
| Side plank (each side) | 15–20 sec |

## Progression

- **Weeks 1–2:** learn the moves and keep it easy.
- **Weeks 3–4:** add 2 reps to each exercise.
- **Month 2 onwards:** move up to 4 rounds, or try harder versions (full push-ups, jump squats).

Stop if you feel sharp pain, chest pain or dizziness.
